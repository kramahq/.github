# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not** open a public issue. Report privately using GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/security-advisories/guidance-on-reporting-and-writing-information-about-vulnerabilities/privately-reporting-a-security-vulnerability)
on the affected repository (Security tab → "Report a vulnerability"), or email **security@kramahq.dev**.

Include the affected version, a description, reproduction steps and the impact you see.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within 3 business days.
- An initial assessment within 7 days, and updates at least every 14 days until resolved.
- Coordinated disclosure: we agree a date with you, publish a fix and an advisory, and credit you unless you prefer otherwise.

## Supported versions

Krama is pre-1.0. Only the latest released version receives security fixes.

## Scope

In scope: the platform, SDK, CLI, first-party packs and their supply chain (installation, consent and permission handling, memory, webhooks).
Out of scope: vulnerabilities in third-party packs or agents, and issues requiring a compromised local machine.
